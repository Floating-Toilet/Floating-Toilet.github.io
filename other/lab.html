Logged in accounts

Note: We have full access to the computers through an universal admin account so individual windows accounts were not apart of the network however this wouldn't stop Google accounts from being logged in.

There was at least one person from the senior class that would leave an account logged in, (Mainly school accounts on google however sometimes we would find personal accounts) every time this would happen we would change a lot of settings and send them an email telling them to sign out.

In the classroom this is where we would be able to access school accounts logged in as a Windows user, again big mistake as we (or the seniors) would mess with every setting available and change the background. My friend possibly ended up being the one who had the most amount of times where he did not log out and it was always hilarious seeing what the seniors did.

This also ends up being an powerful teacher for a unattended desk policy one could find in a business.
